# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, consistent with the user's previous compact demonstration sites and suitable for static deployment.

## Users

Adults considering vocal lessons: complete beginners who feel shy about singing, hobby singers who want more freedom and control, and people preparing a song or performance. The primary visitor is interested but unsure whether their current voice and experience are “enough” to begin.

## Product Purpose

Present Julia Spitsina as a vocal teacher and performing singer, let visitors recognise their goal, understand how lessons can support it, and move to a trial-lesson conversation in Telegram.

## Positioning

Personal vocal work can continue beyond exercises into real creative practice: recording songs, student gatherings and performances with live musicians when that matches the student's goal.

## Operating Context

Lessons are offered online and offline in Krasnodar. A visitor may come to start singing, enjoy a creative hobby, understand their voice, develop existing skill, prepare a performance or record a song.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action: contact Julia in Telegram about a trial lesson.
- The site may show an interactive goal selector using confirmed visitor goals and source-grounded student stories.
- No local lesson or performance video is available; do not imitate a video demonstration.
- Prices, lesson duration, schedule, exact address, package structure and whether the trial is free are unknown.
- Concert and recording participation terms are unknown; present them as documented possibilities, not inclusions or guarantees.
- Russian language, responsive web, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Юлия Спицина. Voice: direct, warm and adult; inviting without promising instant transformation. Use the supplied portrait photography as the visual authority. The site must have a distinct palette and composition from the Elmira methodology site.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors recognise themselves before explaining technique.
- Show creative practice as an available path, never as pressure to perform.
- Use concrete sourced stories instead of generic transformation claims.
- Keep the first step honest: ask about conditions rather than inventing price or duration.
